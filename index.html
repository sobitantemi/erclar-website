The problem

Most businesses don’t have a technology problem. They have a workflow problem.

Work is copied from one spreadsheet to another. Approvals sit in someone’s inbox. Reports are built by hand every month. A new tool is bought, and half the team keeps working the old way.

Another piece of software rarely fixes this. The way the work flows has to change first.

Advice and delivery, from one team.

Many firms advise and then leave you with a report. Others build tools without asking whether the process behind them makes sense. Erclar does the whole job. We redesign the process, build the solution, train your people and stay accountable for the result.
