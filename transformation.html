Erclar Transformation

One programme to redesign a process, build the solution and make sure your team runs it.

Who it is for

Erclar Transformation is for businesses that are growing, or stuck, because of how their work is organised. If your people spend hours on repetitive tasks, if information lives in too many places, or if mistakes keep returning, this is where we begin.

It works for one department or one process at a time. You don’t need a company-wide programme to start.

What you get

  • A clear map of how the process works today and where it loses time and money
  • A redesigned workflow and an approved scope for the solution
  • A working, tested solution in your own environment
  • Dashboards that show results against the goals we agreed at the start
  • A trained team and a clear handover
  • A review of results at 30 and 90 days [confirm before publishing]

The five stages in detail

01

Map

We sit with the people who do the work. You explain the process, and we find the delays, repeated steps, handoffs and errors. You receive a short summary of what we found and what to fix first.

02

Redesign

We turn your goals and the mapped process into a defined solution. You get a future-state process, a clear scope of what is and isn’t included, the systems involved, a phased plan and the measures of success.

03

Automate

We configure, build and connect the solution, and test it with real scenarios before launch. This is where process automation, data and reporting, and AI solutions are put to work, depending on what your process needs.

04

Embed

We train each role in your team, give them simple guides and hand over with a checklist. We stay close through the first weeks so the new workflow becomes the normal one.

05

Evolve

Businesses change, and workflows should too. With an Erclar Care plan we keep the solution running, fix issues, make improvements and show you where to improve next.
